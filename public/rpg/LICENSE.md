# RPG tile/character art — licensing

The pixel-art tilesets and character sprites used by the Adventure (RPG) mode
are from the **Ninja Adventure Asset Pack** by **pixel-boy & AAA**
(https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack), released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** — public domain, free for commercial use,
no attribution required. Credit is given here voluntarily with thanks; it is a
gorgeous pack.

- `tilesets/nj-world.png` — outdoor master tileset (houses, trees, cliffs,
  paths, fences, props). Mirrored from the CC0 pack via
  github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs (`ninja-adventure/`).
- `tilesets/nj-interior.png`, `tilesets/nj-wall.png` — interior floors and
  room-frame walls, from github.com/pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure (`content/map/`).
- `tilesets/nj-char-*.png` — 16 px characters, 4 columns (down/up/left/right)
  × walk frames.
- `facesets/nj-face-*.png` — 38 px dialogue portraits matching the characters.

Earlier versions used Kenney's CC0 "Tiny Town" / "Tiny Dungeon" sheets
(https://kenney.nl) — thanks to Kenney as well.

Water and tall-grass sway are still rendered procedurally on top of the tiles.
Pokémon battle sprites come from PokéAPI (fan/educational use), as elsewhere in
the app; Pokémon overworld sprites are intentionally **not** used.
