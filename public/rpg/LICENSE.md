# RPG tile/character art — licensing

The pixel-art tilesets used by the Adventure (RPG) mode are by **Kenney**
(https://kenney.nl) and released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** —
public domain, free for commercial use, no attribution required. Credit is given
here voluntarily with thanks.

- `tilesets/tiny-town.png` — Kenney "Tiny Town" (CC0). Outdoor tiles.
- `tilesets/tiny-dungeon.png` — Kenney "Tiny Dungeon" (CC0). Interior tiles + characters.

Original per-pack license texts are kept alongside as `*-License.txt`.

Water and tall grass are rendered procedurally (not from these atlases).
Pokémon battle sprites come from PokéAPI (fan/educational use), as elsewhere in
the app; Pokémon overworld sprites are intentionally **not** used.
